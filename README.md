# Taqarrub

An installable prayer, Quran and duas app for the phone. Open [wajeehk.github.io/taqarrub](https://wajeehk.github.io/taqarrub/) in Chrome, then **Add to Home screen**.

Taqarrub brings together the parts of [Sadiq](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greentech.qibla) and [Quran.com](https://quran.com) that matter day to day: prayer times, a Qibla compass, the Quran, and a small set of authentic duas.

## Included

- **Home** — next prayer countdown, today’s times, Hijri date, daily verse, Friday Al-Kahf reminder, Monday/Thursday sunnah-fast note, last-read
- **Quran** — all 114 surahs and 30 juz from the [Quran.com API](https://api.quran.com/api/v4), Arabic (Uthmani) with Sahih International, word-by-word, Ibn Kathir tafsir (English, on demand), audio with reciter choice, search, bookmarks and last-read
- **Prayer** — times from the [Aladhan API](https://aladhan.com/prayer-times-api) (geolocation, London fallback), calculation method and Hanafi/standard Asr, Suhur/Iftar, Tahajjud (last third of the night), commonly cited forbidden times, live Qibla compass
- **Duas** — a small authentic set (Hisnul Muslim / well-known hadith, plus Quranic duas). Every entry has a source. Nothing is invented
- **More** — 99 Names of Allah (Aladhan asmaAlHusna), bookmarks, theme (light / dark / system), location and calculation settings

Times and compass bearings are calculated. They are not a fatwa.

## Also in this version

- Nearby mosques from OpenStreetMap (Overpass), with distance and a link to Google Maps
- Prayer and reminder notifications, with an honest note about PWA limits; scheduled triggers are used when the browser supports them
- Mission and goals (stored only on your phone, no streaks)
- Islamic reminders: morning and evening adhkar, Al-Kahf on Friday, Al-Mulk at night, Ayyam al-Beed
- Hijri / Gregorian calendar with key Islamic dates
- Share verses and duas as an image
- Full Hisnul Muslim (267 duas, 132 chapters, each with a source)
- Mushaf page mode, word-by-word audio, extra translations and tafsir, verse-range playback with highlight, juz / hizb / page navigation, and notes on bookmarks

## Skipped in this first version

- A home-screen prayer widget (not available to a web app on Android)
- A guaranteed always-on adhan when the app is fully closed (a browser limit; see Settings)
- The 70+ reciter catalogue is whatever the Quran.com API returns, not a private library

Audio, translations, tafsir and prayer times need a network connection. The app shell and the duas / 99 Names work offline after the first visit.
